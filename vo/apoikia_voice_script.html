How to record

  • One voice per sitting: do a whole character's lines in a row.
  • Quiet room, phone or mic about a hand's width from your mouth, a little off to the side.
  • Leave half a second of silence before and after; I trim them.
  • Any format works (.ogg .wav .mp3, even a phone's .m4a: send it and I convert). Name it exactly as the file column says.
  • Say lines in Kreyol as written; the part in brackets is the subtitle, not to read.
  • The man_* folders are six different men (deep, rough, young, tired, loud, quiet): one voice each, short lines.
  • Save each take with the file name under its line (the part after the folder is the file; the folder is the speaker). Send them however is easiest; I file them with tools/vo_intake.py.
